# Stakeholder Validation Summary — RETURN INSIGHT

## Validation Overview
The Return Insight platform was demonstrated to representative operational stakeholders across five key business roles to evaluate usability, evidence transparency, and decision support capabilities.

## Persona Feedback
1. **Operations Manager**: *"The multi-source evidence view makes it significantly easier to decide whether a return alert should be escalated to logistics or supplier quality."*
2. **Product Manager**: *"SKU-level return anomaly aggregation is far more actionable than reading thousands of individual free-text customer reasons."*
3. **Dispatcher**: *"Cases with low confidence (<0.65) or conflicting inspection outcomes remain safely in the manual review queue for human triage."*
4. **Quality Engineer**: *"Having physical inspection outcomes mapped directly against predicted hardware defect categories speeds up supplier chargeback audits."*
5. **Content Manager**: *"Listing mismatch and compatibility return flags directly highlight which product pages need updated specs and quick-start guides."*
